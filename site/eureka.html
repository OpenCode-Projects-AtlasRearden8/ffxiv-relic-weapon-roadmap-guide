Stormblood — Eureka Weapons: An Island Adventure With a Serious Rabbit Hole

The only relic line that lives inside its own recurring wilderness zone. Famously social, surprisingly grindy, with a pandas-and-progress-bar economy all its own.

The line in one breath

Route: base weapon (Anemos) → +1 → +2 → Anemos → Pagos → +1 → Elemental → +1 → +2 → Pyros → Hydatos → +1 → Base Eureka → Eureka → Physeos (final IL 405). No Poetics. No Seals. Instead: zone crystals from FATEs, Elemental Levels inside the Forbidden Land, and magic-charging Logos actions. It is a season-long side hobby that famously produces either best friends or burns out; community trackers keep it humane.

What "Eureka" actually asks of you

  • You de-level to 70 inside the zone. Your greater gear is synced, but your
    • substats/materia survive. Elemental Level (a zone-only progression to 60) and armor matter more than your level-100 weapon.
  • FATEs spam all the crystals. Join the "train" (a gaggle of players cycling Notorious
    • Monsters). Gold rating on FATEs is the income.
  • Logos actions (Pyros-onward) are recipes rolled from memory-mote drops; unlocking
    • 30 of them is a stage requirement.
  • The Baldesion Arsenal (Hydatos' 56-man raid) is the final gym: Physeos needs 100
    • Eureka Fragments and a full clear drops ~28.

The journey, step by step (per weapon)

  1. Base artifact (IL 335): job quest weapon in hand, then 100x Protean Crystals.
  2. +1 / +2 (IL 340/345): 400x, then 800x Protean Crystals.
  3. Anemos (IL 355): 3x Pazuzu's Feathers (gold on the Wail in the Willows FATE).
  4. Pagos (IL 360): 5x Frosted Protean Crystals (from the Crystal Forge's vitiated
    5. aether).
  5. Pagos +1 (IL 365): 10x Frosted + 500x Pagos Crystals.
  6. Elemental (IL 370): 16x Frosted + 5x Louhi's Ice.
  7. Elemental +1 / +2 (IL 375/380): 150/200x Pyros Crystals + 10/20 Logos actions
    8. unlocked.
  8. Pyros (IL 385): 300x Pyros Crystals + 5x Penthesilea's Flame (30 Logos total).
  9. Hydatos (IL 390): 50x Hydatos Crystals. +1 (IL 395): 100x. Base Eureka (IL 400):
    10. 100x — this is the final look with customizable substats.
  10. Eureka (IL 405): 100x Hydatos Crystals + 5x Crystalline Scales (Provenance
    11. Watcher only).
  11. Physeos (IL 405, true final): 100x Eureka Fragments — about **4 Baldesion
    12. Arsenal clears**, with the community organizing runs every evening. Elemental bonus makes it BiS inside Eureka zones themselves.

Pair it with roulettes and dailies

  • Eureka time is zone time, so pair it with ... nothing scheduled. Do roulettes
    • before entering, not after — Eureka runs on server-time FATE spawns and eats evenings.
  • The Wondrous Tails weekly is your outside-Eura anchor so the rest of your week
    • stays on rails.
  • Collectible snipers: the zone's NMs double as mincing for mount drops; a single
    • evening of tracking often pays for itself in both crystals and collectibles.

Survival checklist

  • Unlock And We Shall Call it Eureka (lvl 70, Kugane NPC) + take the job-quest artifact weapon
  • Anemos: 1,300x Protean Crystals + 3x Pazuzu's Feathers → Anemos weapon (IL 355)
  • Pagos: 31x Frosted Protean + 500x Pagos Crystals + 5x Louhi's Ice → Elemental
  • Pyros: 650x Pyros Crystals + 30 Logos actions + 5x Penthesilea's Flame
  • Hydatos: 350x Hydatos Crystals + 5x Crystalline Scales → Base Eureka → Eureka (IL 405)
  • Physeos: ~4 Baldesion Arsenal clears (100 Eureka Fragments)
  • Join the train; invest in a tracker/tab-keeping rhythm before each session
  • Set a session limit: like the zone's enemies, relic fatigue crits when you ignore it

Real-life survival notes

  • Anemos → Pagos is the reputation-ruining grind. If a session isn't fun, the zone
    • tolerates absences. Months-long casual pacing is normal.
  • The community trackers that monitor FATE spawns are effectively required
    • conveniences — see Gadgets and Tricks.
  • Elemental Level progress and crystals both persist forever; there is almost never a
    • reason to marathon. Log in, do a train lap, log out, feel excellent.
