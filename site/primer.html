The Relic Primer: Read This Before You Grind Anything

A cozy manual, not an exam. Take a breath. There is no wrong starting point.

Relic weapons are FFXIV's long-running "forge a legendary weapon slowly" system. Every expansion got its own line, so there are now six separate journeys, each with a different personality. This primer teaches you the parts they all share, so each expansion page can just tell you what makes that one special.

What a relic journey actually looks like

Almost every line, no matter the expansion, is built from the same five verbs:

  1. Collect a thing in the world — stone fragments, memories, crystals. Usually FATEs,
    2. duties, or field-op activities.
  2. Hand in materials — bought with Allagan Tomestones of Poetics, crafted, or pulled
    3. from the Market Board.
  3. Run a checklist — books, memories, or crystals that ask you to do specific FATEs,
    4. dungeons, and trials.
  4. Choose a stat or a look — one final customization step.
  5. Repeat a little, or a lot — modern lines are friendlier to alts thanks to
    6. one-time stepping stones, while older lines make you earn every weapon again.

The three currencies: time, tomes, and gil

  • Time is the real cost. Most lines are 10–40+ hours per weapon. The pages ahead give
    • heroes-by-playstyle tables (casual / steady / sprint).
  • Poetics are the workhorse tomestone. Nearly every line eats Poetics by the
    • thousands, and daily roulettes regenerate them for free. Build the habit first.
  • Gil is optional but convenient. Some stages happily accept Market Board purchases.
    • Depending on the line and how much you farm instead of buying, a full weapon costs anywhere from a few hundred thousand gil to a few million.

House rules for a healthy relic journey

  • Roulettes first, relics second. Do your dailies with the relic objective in mind and
    • you finish both. Do relics first and the dailies become an afterthought you resent.
  • Stop while it is still fun. The grind does not expire. Logging off at 70% of a step
    • is a victory, not a failure.
  • One line at a time, one weapon at a time. Spreading three weapon half-journeys
    • across three expansions is how relic fatigue happens.
  • Weekend warrior is a legal playstyle. Every line in this guide can be finished with
    • two or three weekends of steady effort each month.
  • Check patch notes. Step requirements get softened over time and tomestone vendors
    • rotate. Numbers here are estimates as of patch 7.5x — verify big spends in game.

Start with Manderville, that's the trick

Because Manderville costs only Poetics and ~9–14 hours, it is the perfect training wheel run. You learn how relic stepping works without betting a crafting degree or a fortune. After that, any of the others will feel like an expansion of its own.
