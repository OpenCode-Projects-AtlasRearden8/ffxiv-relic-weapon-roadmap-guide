Pairing Relic Steps With Roulettes, Tomestones, and Collectibles

The Golden rule of relic life: never do a step twice that one routine can pay for once.

The daily engine (runs every line)

Once per day, the first run of each roulette pays bonus tomes — and every one of the six relic lines overlaps at least one of these:

Daily pickWhat it paysWhich relic lines dream of it
Alliance RaidsHefty Poetics + raid sealsManderville, Anima, Zodiac (tomes), Resistance (Crystal Tower Loathsomes)

Plus the supporting cast:

  • Wondrous Tails (weekly): insurance tomes + XP; a lifesaver on low-energy weeks.
  • Hunt trains: Poetics via seal exchange, and Company Seals for Anima items.
  • Beast-tribe dailies: seals, gil, scrips, and general "one more currency" income.
  • In the Occult Crescent (Phantom), daily bonus roulettes feed aetherwells directly;
    • in Bozja (Resistance), 1/day from Leveling counts toward Recollection.

Per-line pairing cheat sheet

Zodiac (ARR)

  • Atma/Animus/Nexus bookkeeping is pure ritual: **Leveling + 50/60/70/80 while checking
    • FATE maps**, and bonus-window light farming during Nexus/Zeta.
  • Novus: never skip Brangwine's free daily map after a High-level roulette run.

Anima (HW)

  • Entire line buys with Poetics + Seals: Alliance Raids, hunt trains, beast tribes.
  • Reconditioned's Crystal Sand can be paid in purple crafting/gathering scrips —
    • run a gather-circuit while queues pop.

Eureka (SB)

  • The one true "zone time" line. Set outside-Eureka roulettes before entering, then
    • spend the evening with the NMs-train community.

Resistance (ShB)

  • Leveling roulette = one Bitter Memory per day during Recollection.
  • Crystal Tower alliance raids each pay a Loathsome Memory during Law's Order.
  • Bozja rank progresses passively through CEs while you wait for anything.

Manderville (EW)

  • Nothing but Poetics: your standard daily set pays for a full weapon every ~3 sessions.
  • The daily 50/60/70/80 bonus (and Main Scenario's giant bonus) carries most players.

Phantom (DT)

  • Aetherwell Array: daily-bonus roulettes or bust (450–850 aether per day per well-typed
    • bonus run).
  • Crystal paste, dispellers: Crescent CEs at night, Pilgrim's Traverse as the
    • low-expectation fallback, tomestone-capped duties round out the loop.

Collectible-shaped detours (a.k.a. "I should stop, but...")

Relic journeys and collectible hunting share an economy: FATE-heavy steps double as mount-drop farming (the dog-walking Atma loop is secretly a St34 mount lottery), Crescent treasure coffers, orchestrion rolls from the Mhach/Ivalice alliance raids that feed Resistance memories, and Eureka NM achievements. Set a side-quest allowance of about 20% of each session, and you'll collect without the grind creeping.

A simple weekly template that fits a life

  1. Mon–Fri (≤1h): daily roulette set; bank Poetics; keep whatever quest line is active
    2. moving in 15-minute dribbles.
  2. One long weekend block (2–4h): the "actual step" — Bozja rank, Crescent CEs, books,
    3. or an unsynced dungeon tour.
  3. Sunday (optional): Wondrous Tails + hunt train to top off whatever currency the
    4. coming week will eat.
