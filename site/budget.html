Budget philosophy (the three rules)

  1. Buy only when the step is open. Tomes and mats stockpiled "in advance" often get
    2. cheaper, or get reduced in quantity requirement, by the time you arrive.
  2. Crash the cheapest currency. There is always a version of each step paid in the
    3. currency you overflow with — Poetics rain? Anima and Manderville. Seals overflow? Anima again. Bicolor gemstones? Phantom's siderite disappears.
  3. Treat MB prices as weather, not climate. Soul-glazed relic-era materia and relic
    4. synthetics swing wildly week to week; a 5-minute scan before buying beats 500k gil.

That first-weapon premium

Every modern line (Resistance, Manderville, Phantom, Anima, Zodiac) prices a good chunk of its journey as one-time per account or per line: Resistance's two memory grinds, Phantom's Knowledge Crystal, Zodiac Zeta→Anima skip, Anima's replica unlocks. The second weapon in any line is dramatically faster — so pick a job you love for weapon #1, and let the sequel momentum carry the alts you merely like.
