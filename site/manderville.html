Endwalker — Manderville Weapons: The Five-Step Victory Lap

The friendliest relic line ever shipped. Pure Poetics, a Hildibrand comedy show attached, and about two weeks of dailies. This is the "your first relic" recommendation.

The line in one breath

Four stages: Manderville (IL 615) → Amazing (630) → Majestic (645) → Mandervillous (665). Every stage is the same move: 3 items from Jubrunnah in Radz-at-Han, each 500 Poetics (1,500 Poetics per stage; 6,000 total per weapon). Confederate-friendly, utterly cheerful, and the attributes step lets you pick crit/det tuning like a real endgame weapon might.

Prerequisites (the only "hurdle")

  • Level 90 in a combat job, Endwalker MSQ complete.
  • The entire ARR Hildibrand line through The Imperfect Gentleman (Gilgamesh
    • included), then Somehow Further Hildibrand Adventures as stages advance — Generational Bonding before Amazing, Not from Around Here before Majestic, and Gentlemen at Heart (which includes The Gilded Araya trial) before Mandervillous.
  • That's it. No field zone, no maps, no tears.

The journey, step by step

  1. Manderville (IL 615): Make It a Manderville — 3x Manderium Meteorite.
  2. Amazing (IL 630): 3x Complementary Chondrite.
  3. Majestic (IL 645): 3x Amplifying Achondrite. Weapon attributes unlock here —
    4. choose two maxed stats + one low; re-tune later at the Alchemist for one extra item.
  4. Mandervillous (IL 665, final): 3x Cosmic Crystallite (+ 1 spare if you may
    5. re-tune stats).

Pair it with roulettes and dailies

  • This is the roulettes line. A typical day: Alliance Raids + Leveling +
    • 50/60/70/80 + Main Scenario roulettes nets roughly 2,000–2,500 Poetics, so a weapon is ~3 decent daily sessions if you start with a stockpile... or ~10 relaxed days cold.
  • Beyond daily roulettes: hunt trains, the Wondrous Tails weekly (the salve for a
    • stalled tome day), and your HW beast-tribe dailies all trickle Poetics.
  • Both Collectibles → the same place: Poetics buys gear at Sundry Splendors and
    • level-49/50 etc. upgrade caches, so leftover tomes keep paying across the whole line.

Survival checklist

  • Finish the Hildibrand chain through The Imperfect Gentleman (yes, all of it — get the popcorn)
  • Make It a Manderville + 3x Manderium Meteorite (1,500 Poetics)
  • Somehow Further Hildibrand steps as they gate stages
  • Amazing: 3x Complementary Chondrite (1,500 Poetics)
  • Majestic: 3x Amplifying Achondrite (1,500 Poetics); pick your two favorite stats
  • Mandervillous: 3x Cosmic Crystallite (1,500 Poetics)
  • Optional: 1 extra item of each stat-material grade if you want to re-tune at the Alchemist
  • Take glamour photos. That's the whole reward, and it's a good one.

Real-life survival notes

  • One stage per session-end rule works miracles: **log out holding an unopened purchase
    • goal** and you'll never feel behind.
  • Buy each stage's 3 items as soon as the quest opens (cheapest step in the game), then
    • spend your time on the Hildibrand comedy instead of grinding.
  • It's also the ideal "test drive" for relic life in general — if even this grates on you,
    • you'll know to space out the other lines instead of bingeing multiple at once.
