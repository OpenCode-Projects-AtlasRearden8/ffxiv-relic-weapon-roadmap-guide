Hidden Tricks, Timers, and Community Gadgets

The unofficial survival kit. Everything here is public/community tooling and in-game quality-of-life; no forbidden magic, no game files, no automation of the game client.

Timers and trackers (the real "gadget shelf")

  • FATE/CE trackers for field zones. Community web tools track Eureka NM spawns,
    • Bozjan/Zadnor Critical Engagements, and Occult Crescent activities in real time. during Atma/Animus-era relics the same idea covers spawn-dependent book FATEs.
  • Light-bonus trackers (Zodiac). Nexus and Zeta light farming runs on 2-hour bonus
    • windows for specific old duties; trackers call out which duty is hot and keep you from running the wrong one for an hour.
  • Universalis (market data): check before any big materia/craft purchase. Novus is a
    • live gil-minigame if you comparison-shop across grades.
  • Teamcraft and similar lists: build a shopping manifest for a whole relic line and
    • check items off as you stockpile. Also the honest answer to "how far am I really?"
  • Lodestone/achievement pages: your own relic progress across jobs is browser-readable;
    • handy for second-weapon planning.

In-game tricks players never mention

  • Bonus-window route logic (Zodiac Zodiac stage): Nexus light is faster on old
    • single-trial duties than on dungeons. Two hours with a tracker beats ten hours blind.
  • Weapon-in-bag vs. weapon-equipped: Anima's Complete step only needs the weapon
    • carried (matching job), not equipped, for dungeon hand-ins — but the density grind needs it equipped. Read the requirement sentence twice; it saves a whole mis-queue.
  • Exit-portal timing (Anima Awoken): the "equipped at exit" check often trips people
    • who swap to comfort gear at the final pull. Finish the bosses, then re-equip.
  • Leveling roulette memory (Resistance)： the daily bonus memory lands on a random
    • Leveling dungeon — take the freebie, then cover the gap with synced HW dungeon runs.
  • Aetherwell economics (Phantom): first-run daily bonuses pay 450–850 aether; unbonused
    • repeats pay pocket change. One well per day, four days, done.
  • Knowledge Crystal minimap (Phantom): marked enemies show a crescent symbol above
    • their names — follow the map pictures the book gives you instead of hunting maps.
  • Replica shortcuts: Zodiac Zeta in inventory → Drake replicas; Anima IL 230+ →
    • Restoration Node replicas; Blade's → Matte Replicas from Regana. You can glamour your favorite old stage even after upgrading past it.
  • Calamity Salvager insurance: lost base relics are re-purchasable for a pittance
    • (1,000 gil) — never panic-delete progress.

Habits worth more than any tool

  • The stockpile-first rule. Before opening a stage, gather currency to cover it.
    • Nothing stalls a relic like realizing Reconditioned has a tomes-shaped mouth.
  • Two-hour session cap on FATE lines. Atma, Anima crystals, and Zadnor memories are
    • all "the longer you stay, the worse it feels." Chip in, stop on any drop, repeat.
  • Queues as tomestone dispensers. Long DPS queues are Poetics-earning time whether
    • or not the relic step cares; bank whatever the run pays.
  • Keep one quest open, not five. Multiple relic quest threads at once is how hobby
    • projects feel like homework.
  • Screenshot the final weapon. Every line ends with a "set attributes / obtain
    • weapon" click and then... anticlimax. A screenshot makes the grind feel paid off.
  • Log your progress in a checklist — the HTML site in this repo saves ticks in your
    • browser, so next session starts with "oh right, I only need 2 more memories."
