A cozy, plain-English manual for the six relic weapon journeys in Final Fantasy XIV — what each one actually takes, how long it takes, what it costs, and how to finish one with your real life still intact.

How to use it

Each expansion page has:

  • The line in one breath — what it is and who it suits.
  • The journey, step by step — what each stage genuinely asks for.
  • Pair it with — which roulettes, tomestones, or collectible hunts plug straight in.
  • A checklist — so you always know where you stand when you log back in.
  • Real-life survival notes — small habits so the grind stays fun.

A note on numbers

Time and gil figures are honest ballparks for a steady, casual-friendly playstyle, based on the state of the game around patch 7.5x. Square Enix softens requirements over time, so treat every number as a starting point and verify big spends in game.
