*Estimates for one final-stage weapon, played at a steady casual pace, as of patch 7.5x.
Square Enix nerfs requirements over time — treat these as mood-setting, not gospel, and
verify any beefy purchase in game.*

## Budget philosophy (the three rules)

1. **Buy only when the step is open.** Tomes and mats stockpiled "in advance" often get
   cheaper, or get *reduced in quantity requirement*, by the time you arrive.
2. **Crash the cheapest currency.** There is always a version of each step paid in the
   currency you overflow with — Poetics rain? Anima and Manderville. Seals overflow?
   Anima again. Bicolor gemstones? Phantom's siderite disappears.
3. **Treat MB prices as weather, not climate.** Soul-glazed relic-era materia and relic
   synthetics swing wildly week to week; a 5-minute scan before buying beats 500k gil.

## That first-weapon premium

Every modern line (Resistance, Manderville, Phantom, Anima, Zodiac) prices a good chunk of
its journey as **one-time per account or per line**: Resistance's two memory grinds,
Phantom's Knowledge Crystal, Zodiac Zeta→Anima skip, Anima's replica unlocks. The second
weapon in any line is dramatically faster — so pick a job you love for weapon #1, and let
the sequel momentum carry the alts you merely like.
