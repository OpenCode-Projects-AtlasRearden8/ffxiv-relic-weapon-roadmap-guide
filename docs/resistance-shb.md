# Shadowbringers — Resistance Weapons: The Story-Forward Steady Grinder

*Bozja and Zadnor are big war zones with a storyline attached; the relic line makes you
participate. Extremely alt-friendly DROP economics — memories drop regardless of your job.**

## The line in one breath

Route: **Resistance (IL 485) → Augmented (500) → Recollection (500, glow) → Law's Order
(510) → Augmented Law's Order (515) → Blade's (535, glow)**. Two one-time "account-level"
memories grinds; every stage before/after is per-weapon but memory drops are shared, so
the second weapon is dramatically faster.

## The journey, step by step

### 0. Beyond the shadow of a doubt: Bozja ranks first
You'll live in the **Bozjan Southern Front** (and later **Zadnor**). Skirmishes and
Critical Engagements hand out zone rank, which matters — Rank 10 in Bozja is a hard gate
before Law's Order. Run CEs even when grinding something else; rank never regresses.

### 1. Resistance (IL 485)
Level 80 + ShB MSQ + *The City of Lost Angels*, then the quest chain through *Fire in the
Forge* — the first weapon is literally free. Subsequent weapons: **4x Thavnairian
Scalepowder** (250 Poetics each = **1,000 Poetics**).

### 2. Augmented (IL 500)
*For Want of a Memory:* **20x each** of Tortured / Sorrowful / Harrowing Memories of the
Dying. Sources: Bozja skirmishes and CEs (random), **HW-zone FATEs at gold (100%
guaranteed drops)** — actually the fastest solo route — or cooperative "omnifarm" clusters.

### 3. Recollection (IL 500) — this is where the glow arrives
*The Will to Resist:* **6x Bitter Memories**. One per day from the **Leveling roulette**;
the rest from synced 4-man HW dungeons (The Antitower is the popular speed pick) and
Bozja CEs. The Leveling roulette pairing makes this the single friendliest step in the
line.

### 4. Law's Order (IL 510)
Gates: Bozja **Rank 10**, clear *The Battle of Castrum Lacus Litore*, and the story
through *Delubrum Reginae*. Then *Change of Arms:* **15x Loathsome Memories** (5 per
Castrum run; 1 each from Crystal Tower alliance raids; CE drops). Castrum is the honest
route: ~1 per hour in an active zone.

### 5. The one-time grinds (do each once per account)
*The Resistance Remembers* + Zadnor unlock quests (account-wide, once): 30x each of
Compact Axles/Springs (Zadnor zone 1), 30x each of "A Day in the Life…" battletomes,
and 30x each of Bleak/Lurid Memories — plus 18x Haunting and 18x Vexatious Memories
(Mhach and Ivalice alliance raids, Gyr Abania zone FATEs). Gets shorter every year as
unsynced raid farming improves.

### 6. Augmented Law's Order (IL 515)
*A New Path of Resistance:* **15x Timeworn Artifacts** per weapon. Delubrum Reginae
pays 3 per run (or PotD deep-floor drops). This repeat is the quality-of-life place to
spend Bozja story time.

### 7. Blade's (IL 535, glow) — the last stop
*Irresistible:* **15x Raw Emotions** per weapon, from **The Dalriada** (3/CE), Delubrum
(2/run), synced SB dungeons like Hells' Lid, or Heaven-on-High. Grab the free Matte
Replica afterward for the no-glow version.

## Costs at a glance (per weapon)

| What | Rough total |
| --- | --- |
| Poetics | 1,000 (repeat base quest) + 400 if you re-roll stats at Allagan Node |
| Gil | ~0 round trip (it's a Bozja economy) |
| Time | **20–35 hours first weapon** (casual 4–6 weeks, sprint ~1 week); alts faster |
| Bozja Rank math | Rank 10 in Bozja is a real gate — no shortcuts |

## Pair it with roulettes and dailies

- **The Leveling roulette** is literally a Bitter Memory printer (1/day during Recollection).
- **Crystal Tower alliance raids** pay a Loathsome Memory each — queue Labyrinth of the
  Ancients while the line is on Law's Order.
- **Hunt trains + Wondrous Tails** on the side keep Poetics income alive for Scalepowder.
- Collectibles: Bozja/zeta relic mats are all drops, but the Mhach/Ivalice alliance raids
  that feed the one-time memories grind double as orchestrion roll and collectible farms.

## Survival checklist

- [ ] Unlock Gangos (lvl 80, ShB MSQ, *The City of Lost Angels*) and finish the intro chain
- [ ] First Resistance weapon earned; repeat quests unlocked via 4x Scalepowder (1,000 Poetics)
- [ ] Augmented: 20+20+20 Memories of the Dying (Bozja CEs or HW-FATE golds)
- [ ] Recollection: 6x Bitter Memories (1/day from Leveling roulette + synced HW dungeons)
- [ ] Law's Order gates: Bozja Rank 10 + Castrum Lacus Litore + story through Delubrum Reginae
- [ ] Law's Order: 15x Loathsome Memories (Castrum route recommended)
- [ ] One-time memories grinds complete (Axles/Springs, A Day in the Life, Bleak/Lurid, Haunting/Vexatious)
- [ ] Augmented Law's Order: 15x Timeworn Artifacts per weapon (Delubrum route)
- [ ] Blade's: 15x Raw Emotions per weapon (Dalriada/Delubrum/Hells' Lid/HoH)
- [ ] Claim Matte Replica; keep 2nd weapon on hold only if healthy

## Real-life survival notes

- The **Rank 10 gate** means casual players should treat Bozja rank as a background chore
  from day one — pop into skirmishes even while questing elsewhere.
- Each memory stage is expressly alt-friendly: drops work regardless of the job you're on,
  and per-stage quests can be tracked in parallel for alts (keep quest logs tidy).
- Delubrum Reginae for Timeworn Artifacts is a great **low-brain evening content**:
  queue, zone out, collect.
