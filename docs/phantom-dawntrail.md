# Dawntrail — Phantom Weapons: The Occult Crescent Grand Tour

*The current-expansion line (patches 7.25–7.55). It's tied to the Occult Crescent field
operation, but unusually polite: several steps can be done entirely outside the zone with
roulettes and tomestones.*

## The line in one breath

Five stages: **Penumbrae (IL 745) → Umbrae (760, glow) → Obscurum (775) → Eclipticum
(790) → Occultum (795, glow)**. Big tomestone purchases (Heliometry, then Mathmetics),
a demoitative market-board/craft step or two, plenty of FATEs in the Crescent, one huge
**crystal paste** grind, and the Knowledge-Crystal victory lap at the end. Like Resistance,
second weapons onward are much lighter than the first.

## Prerequisites

- Level 100 combat job + Dawntrail MSQ complete.
- Occult Crescent questline through *Unfamiliar Territory* (keep going further for zone
  access; more of it unlocks the North Horn later).

## The journey, step by step

### 1. Penumbrae (IL 745)
*Arcane Artistry:* gather **3x each of 6 demiatma types** (18 total) — random drops from
FATEs and Critical Encounters in the Crescent's South Horn (or DT-zone FATEs) — then
*Forging the Phantasmal* plus 3x **Arcanite** (500 Heliometry tomes each = 1,500 total).

### 2. Umbrae (IL 760) — glow arrives
*Keeping the Old Ways Alive:* five hand-ins to Gerolt — **Rroneek glue** (300,000 gil from
Goplu in Tuliyollal), **Ut'ohmu siderite** (600 bicolor gemstones), three **Synthetic Dark
Matters** (crafted from regional folklore/12-mastery materials *or* bought on the Market
Board). Then *Aether, Aether, Everywhere:* fill **four aetherwells with 10,000 aether each**
by queuing the high-level/trial/alliance/normal-raid roulettes — **the daily bonus pays
450–850 aether, so this step IS your daily roulettes for a while** (5+ days of dailies).
Finish with 3x **Waxing Arcanite** (1,500 Heliometry).

### 3. Obscurum (IL 775)
*Timeworn Techniques:* 4 hand-ins — **Umbral clay** (500,000 gil) plus three crafted /
Market-Board items once again. Then *In Pursuit of Perfection:* the grand **1,200 crystal
paste** march, in waves of **100 → 200 → 300 → 600**. Wide-open sources: Crescent
Critical Engagements, the listed roulettes, Pilgrim's Traverse (the deep dungeon), and
high-end duties. This is the step that eats weeks. Finish with 3x **Waning Arcanite**
(1,500 **Mathmetics** tomes).

### 4. Eclipticum (IL 790)
*Under No Illusion:* 4 hand-ins — **Monarch whetstone** (500,000 gil) + three crafted/MB
materials. Then *Phantoms to Fillet:* 100x each of Alpha/Beta/Gamma **Phantom Dispellers**
(FATEs and CEs in the Crescent's **North Horn**, or the listed roulettes). Finish with 3x
**Ecliptic Arcanite** (1,500 Mathmetics).

### 5. Occultum (IL 795, glowing) — the victory lap
*Final Phantasm:* fill the **Knowledge Crystal** with its 8-book checklist — gold-ranked
FATEs across DT zones, every DT leveling and expert dungeon, DT normal trials, Echoes of
Vana'diel alliance raids, and all 12 Arcadion normal raids. Once per character: future
weapons skip it (just the 790→795 tomestone hand-in).

## Pair it with roulettes and dailies

- **The Aetherwell step is designed around dailies** — queue your normal roulette set and
  the step fills itself within a week. Do not farm this stage with unsynced repeats; the
  daily-bonus math wins.
- **Crystal paste** — target the Crescent's Critical Engagements on zone evenings, and
  Pilgrim's Traverse on quiet evenings. Cap past tomes each day too; every listed activity
  outside the zone pays.
- Bicolor gemstone **sandy dailies** (DT regional vendors) cover the siderite on their own.
- Collectibles/collectors: the North Horn dispeller loop doubles as a chance to snag
  Occult treasure, Fortune Cards and zone mementos most evenings.

## Survival checklist

- [ ] Crescent unlocked through *Unfamiliar Territory*; South Horn scout + demiatma farming (18x)
- [ ] *Forging the Phantasmal* + 3x Arcanite (1,500 Heliometry)
- [ ] Umbrae materials: glue (300k gil), siderite (600 bicolor), 3x Synthetic Dark Matter
- [ ] Aetherwells: 4 x 10,000 aether via daily-bonus roulettes (~1 week of dailies)
- [ ] 3x Waxing Arcanite (1,500 Heliometry) → Umbrae (glow!)
- [ ] Obscurum: Umbral clay (500k gil) + 3x crafted materials
- [ ] 1,200 crystal paste in waves (100/200/300/600)
- [ ] 3x Waning Arcanite (1,500 Mathmetics)
- [ ] Eclipticum: Monarch whetstone (500k gil) + 3x materials + 300x Phantom Dispellers + 3x Ecliptic Arcanite (1,500 Mathmetics)
- [ ] Occultum: complete the 8-category Knowledge Crystal once; set your weapon's attributes

## Real-life survival notes

- **Start at the zone, not the paste.** The Crescent's level track (Knowledge levels, FATEs,
  CEs) pays paste, dispellers, demiatma and fun all at once — dailies first, then the zone.
- Second weapons skip the Knowledge Crystal, so your **first weapon is the expensive one
  and every one after is a tomestone errand**. Plan accordingly and don't buy alts' mats
  until you know you want them.
- If you burn out mid-paste, the grind holds your progress forever. Weeks of absence are
  harmless; the tomes and zone are patient.
