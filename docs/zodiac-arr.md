# ARR — Zodiac Weapons: The Long, Legendary Classic

*For players who want the full old-school pilgrimage: gorgeous ornate weapons, a nostalgic
road trip through A Realm Reborn, and the slowest journey of the six. Bring tea.*

## The line in one breath

Eight stages: Relic → Zenith → Atma → Animus → Novus → Nexus → Zodiac (Braves) → Zeta.
Final product is an **IL 135 glamour**. Rewards are the fame, the achievement, and the
best-looking "church organ" weapons in the game. Practically: this line is mostly FATEs,
books, old dungeons, and two light-farming grinds.

**Heads up:** the quests in this chain are mostly one-time, non-repeatable, and
instructional — the quest completes itself when you do the work, and you hand items to the
vendor with the right weapon in your inventory. A finished **Zeta can jump-start your
Anima journey** (trade it in), and completed Zetas also let you buy replicas from Drake.

## The journey, step by step

### 1. The Relic (IL 80)
Unlock *The Weaponsmith of Legend* from **Nedrick Ironheart** in Vesper Bay, then take the
job-specific *A Relic Reborn* from Gerolt in North Shroud. It's an endless-errand quest:
kill marked enemies, run old level-50 duties unsynced, and hand Gerolt a **crafted,
melded job weapon piece** (buyable on the Market Board if you don't craft). Amdapor Keep
and The Wanderer's Palace show up along the way. Finish with 1x **Radz-at-Han Quenching
Oil** (15 Poetics, Auriana in Mor Dhona — buy 3x **Thavnairian Mist** while you're there).

### 2. Zenith (IL 90)
Trade your relic + 3x **Thavnairian Mist** (20 Poetics each = 60 Poetics) at the Furnace
in North Shroud. No quest. Five minutes.

### 3. Atma (IL 100)
Talk to Jalzahn (*Up in Arms*), then wear your Zenith and farm **12 Zodiac Atma** — one per
ARR zone, ~20% drop chance from any completed FATE while your weapon is equipped. Ugly
luck nights happen; two or three zone tours per week is plenty.

### 4. Animus (IL 100)
*Trials of the Braves* from G'jusana (Mor Dhona). Complete **9 books** (100 Poetics each =
900 total), one at a time: kill marked enemies, clear 3 dungeons, some FATEs, some
leves — all with your Atma equipped. Book FATEs are the slow part; check the map, do other
tasks while waiting. One book per hour-ish is realistic at level 100.

### 5. Novus (IL 110)
Buy 3x **Superior Enchanted Ink** (25 Poetics each), get a Sphere Scroll, and infuse **75
Alexandrite** (15x **Mysterious Maps**, 75 Poetics each — a free map daily from Brangwine
after *Morbid Motivation* + a High-level Dungeon run) plus **Grade I–IV materia** (buy
~20 of each grade; failures lose materia, not Alexandrite). This is the big **gil spike**
of the line: Grade I–II materia are surprisingly pricey on many markets.

### 6. Nexus (IL 115)
*Mmmmmm, Soulglazed Relics.* Then farm **2,000 light** with the weapon equipped via bonus
windows (2-hour rotations on old trials/dungeons; check community trackers). Unglamorous,
very podcast-friendly.

### 7. Zodiac / Zodiac Braves (IL 125)
Four sub-quests. Each needs a purchased item (**Bombard Core** 20,000 seals ×2,
**Sacred Spring Water** 200 Poetics ×2 — plus zone items costing ~400,000 gil total if
bought), a handful of unlocked hard dungeons, and HQ crafted "Perfect" parts from the
market board. The sub-quest dungeons overlap heavily with Animus books if you plan ahead.

### 8. Zeta (IL 135)
*Rise and Shine.* Buy 12 **mahatma** (50 Poetics each = 600 Poetics) and awaken them
one at a time in the light-farming duties, then finish at Jalzahn. Congratulations — you
now own the most "yes, I certainly did that" weapon in the game.

## Costs at a glance (per weapon)

| What | Rough total |
| --- | --- |
| Poetics | ~2,000 (oils, inks, maps, water, mahatma) |
| Company Seals | 80,000 (Bombard Cores) |
| Gil (buy-everything path) | ~800k–1.5M (materia, crafted pieces, zone items) |
| Time | **20–31 hours** (casual 3–4 weekends, sprint 4–5 days) |

## Pair it with roulettes and dailies

- **Nexus light farming** and daily roulettes are the same activity. Queue Leveling and
  50/60/70/80 while farming bonus windows — Poetics for Animus/Nexus/Zeta purchases come
  from the same runs.
- **Brangwine's daily** (free Mysterious Map after a High-level roulette) is literally a
  free Alexandrite day. Never skip it.
- Books: wait-for-FATE time is prime **Grand Company hunt curtains** / beast tribe time.

## Survival checklist

- [ ] Unlock *The Weaponsmith of Legend* (Vesper Bay) and Gerolt's *A Relic Reborn*
- [ ] Source the crafted melded job weapon piece (craft or Market Board)
- [ ] Run the old duties and Amdapor Keep; hand in 1x Radz-at-Han Quenching Oil (15 Poetics)
- [ ] Zenith: trade 3x Thavnairian Mist (60 Poetics) at the Furnace
- [ ] Atma: 12 Zodiac Atma from ARR-zone FATEs (weapon equipped)
- [ ] Animus: 9 books complete (900 Poetics)
- [ ] Novus: Sphere Scroll + 75 Alexandrite (15 Mysterious Maps, 1,125 Poetics) + materia
- [ ] Nexus: Soulglaze + 2,000 light via bonus windows
- [ ] Zodiac: 4 sub-quests (Bombard Cores 40k seals ×2, Sacred Spring Water 400 Poetics ×2, zone items, HQ parts)
- [ ] Zeta: 12 mahatma (600 Poetics) awakened, then finish at Jalzahn
- [ ] Save a Zeta, or trade it in later to fast-track Animus

## Real-life survival notes

- Atma and Animus are the motivation cliffs. Do **one book or one zone tour per session**
  and stop. The line is a season, not a sprint.
- It's fully soloable unsynced at level 100 (and even on the free trial). Bring a
  podcast.
- Never delete the Zeta — replicas (and the Anima shortcut) live in your inventory.
