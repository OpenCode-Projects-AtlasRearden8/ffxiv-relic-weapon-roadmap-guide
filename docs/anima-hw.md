# Heavensward — Anima Weapons: Tomestone Cathedral on a Racecar Chassis

*The iconic glowing aether-weapons of the HW era. Long, but unusually friendly to a
"just do my roulettes" playstyle — most of the buys are Poetics and Company Seals.*

## The line in one breath

Seven stages: Animated → Awoken → Anima → Hyperconductive → Reconditioned → Sharpened →
Complete → Lux (final IL 275). Not required for combat anymore, but the**best glamour of
the six** for many players, and — crucially — every animate step is repeatable, so weapon
#2 onward is faster. A finished **Zodiac Zeta skips the first step** for any Anima weapon.

## The journey, step by step

### 1. Animated (IL 170)
*Soul without Life* from Ardashir's line. Either (a) farm **6 Luminous Crystals** (one per
HW zone, ~33% drop from FATEs) to earn Astral + Umbral Nodules, or (b) **hand over one
Zodiac Zeta** and skip it entirely (weapon is destroyed; you gain permanent access to that
Zodiac line's replicas from Drake).

### 2. Awoken (IL 200)
*Toughening Up.* Clear **10 level 50/60 dungeons in a set order** (Snowcloak, two
"straight to Ardashir" beats, Dusk Vigil, Sohm Al, The Aery, The Vault...) unsynced at
level 100 — one evening, weapon equipped when you hit the exit portal.

### 3. Anima (IL 210)
*Coming into Its Own.* Hand Cristiana (Mor Dhona) **10x each of the 4 Unidentifiable
items** (150 Poetics each = **6,000 Poetics**) and **4x each of the 4 Company Seals
items** (5,000 seals each = **80,000 seals**, or craft/Market Board instead).

### 4. Hyperconductive (IL 230)
*Finding Your Voice.* **5x Aether Oil** (350 Poetics each = **1,750 Poetics**). Weapon
gains its signature glow.

### 5. Reconditioned (IL 240)
*A Dream Fulfilled.* The big grind: **~60x Umbrite** (75 Poetics each ≈ **4,300 Poetics**)
plus **~60x Crystal Sand** (Ulan in Idyllshire trades — roughly **6,500 more Poetics** via
Mists+Ink, or ~3,700 purple Crafters'/Gatherers' Scrips if you gather/craft). Tune the mix
to whatever you have more of. This is the widest step; budget it, don't fear it.

### 6. Sharpened (IL 260)
*Future Proof.* **50x Singing Clusters** (40 Poetics each = **2,000 Poetics**), with
quest-top-ups from light-party duties and a weekly quest that pays 18 at once.

### 7. Complete (IL 270)
*Born Again Anima* + *Some Assembly Required.* Clear 3 HW hard dungeons (weapon in bag,
weapon's job), then an aetheric-density zap zap grind with the weapon equipped (old
Alexander savage floors unsynced make this quick). Hand in **15x Pneumite** (100 Poetics
each = **1,500 Poetics**, or 4,000 seals each).

### 8. Lux (IL 275)
*Best Friends Forever.* Clean 12 ARR-era trials in ordered groups (a freebie-fest at
level 100), plus **1x Archaic Enchanted Ink** (500 Poetics). You now have the ethereal
Lux and, honestly, the prettiest glow in the game.

| What | Rough total |
| --- | --- |
| Poetics | ~15,800–16,300 |
| Company Seals | 80,000 (Anima step) + up to 60,000 (Pneumite) |
| Gil (buy-everything path) | ~100k–600k (Seal items off the MB, occasional crafted bits) |
| Scrips (alt path) | ~3,700 purple (Crystal Sand) |
| Time | **30–45 hours** (casual 4–8 weeks, sprint ~2 weeks) |

## Pair it with roulettes and dailies

- **Daily roulettes are the Anima engine.** Alliance Raids, Leveling, 50/60/70/80 and
  Main Scenario pay hundreds of Poetics each; the entire line buys with Poetics and Seals.
- **Beast tribe dailies + raid hop dam** stockpile Company Seals passively; 80,000 is
  two weeks of casual dailies.
- **The Antitower trick is real:** synced HW dungeons like The Antitower speed Recollection-style runs, and for *Complete*'s density grind, unsynced
  old raid floors (A9S x ~21) are the speedrun; otherwise old dungeons work.
- Collectible lovers: purple **crafting/gathering scrips** (Wondrous Tails! custom
  deliveries!) pay for Reconditioned's Crystal Sand so you can skip some Poetics.

## Survival checklist

- [ ] Unlock via *An Unexpected Proposal*; get incremented nodules (6 Luminous Crystals) **or** trade a Zeta
- [ ] Awoken: 10 dungeons in order, unsynced, weapon equipped at the exit
- [ ] Anima: 40 Unidentifiables (6,000 Poetics) + 16 Seal items (80,000 seals)
- [ ] Hyperconductive: 5x Aether Oil (1,750 Poetics)
- [ ] Reconditioned: ~60 Umbrite (4,500 Poetics) + ~60 Crystal Sand (~6,750 Poetics or ~3,700 scrips)
- [ ] Sharpened: 50 Singing Clusters (2,000 Poetics)
- [ ] Complete: 3 HW hard dungeons + density grind + 15 Pneumite (1,500 Poetics or 60,000 seals)
- [ ] Lux: 12-trial tour + 1x Archaic Enchanted Ink (500 Poetics)
- [ ] Stockpile in advance: 15k Poetics + 140k Seals (or plan a 3–4 week filler schedule)

## Real-life survival notes

- Reconditioned (step 5) is the motivation canyon. Split it: **Umbrite one week, Crystal
  Sand the next**, and don't buy any of it until Reconditioned is LIVE on your quest log.
- Every stage from Anima onward is a repeatable quest — alts and second weapons are much
  friendlier here than in ARR.
- The **Restoration Node** in Idyllshire sells replica glamour versions of earlier stages
  once you hit IL 230 — you never have to lose your favorite looks.
